Step 1 Goal & Instructions
Describe exactly what you want the AI to do (e.g. "Write a recipe", "Summarize this draft"). Use double curly braces {{like_this}} to insert custom dynamic placeholders.
Define clear rules on what the AI must NOT do (e.g. "Do not mention pricing", "Keep response under 300 words"). You can also click the quick-insert rules below.
Choose the visual structure you want the AI's final response to match (e.g. a table, bullet points, or email).
Step 2 Persona & Context
Assign a specific expertise or character role to the AI to control its tone, vocabulary, and depth (e.g. "SaaS Copywriter", "Calculus Tutor").
Provide background details, reference facts, or project circumstances that the AI needs to know to generate accurate answers.
Step 3 Examples & References
Provide an example of an input and its ideal output. This is the single most effective way to teach the AI the exact style and format you want!
